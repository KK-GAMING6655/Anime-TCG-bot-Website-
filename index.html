Everything your server needs
to run a real card economy.

Gacha Pulls

Pull a single card or up to 20 at once. Bulk pulls sort low-to-high rarity and taper duplicate odds so your binder stays varied.

Six Rarity Tiers

From Common to Super Legendary, every card carries a real drop weight and a distinct color identity across the whole bot.

Trading & Gifting

Trade cards directly with another collector, or gift cards and coins outright — no fees, no friction.

Card Market

List your cards for sale and browse what everyone else is offering — a living marketplace inside your server.

Crates

Coin crates and card crates, given by admins or earned — opened live with an interactive pick-your-crate menu.

Leveling System

Every pull earns XP by rarity. Level up and the bot renders a custom card announcing it — with your avatar baked right in.

Leaderboards

Balance, collection points, or level — each leaderboard renders as a real image, avatars and all, switchable with a dropdown.

Full Economy

Daily and beg income, coin toss bets, and a public/private profile toggle to control who can see your stats.
